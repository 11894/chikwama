How this forecast is built — model pipeline (demo)

1. Transaction ingestion
Mobile money (Airtel Money / TNM Mpamba) and till transactions are pulled with the owner's consent and categorised into inflows and outflows.
2. Pattern detection
The model learns each business's seasonal rhythm — pay days, restock cycles, school-fee months — from its own history.
3. Forward simulation
Known upcoming obligations (loan instalments, supplier invoices) are combined with the learned pattern to simulate the next 30 days of balance.
4. Action matching
If a shortfall is predicted, the system ranks interventions — from free (shift a payment date) to financial (short-term facility) — by how much runway each buys.

Why this matters for NBM — the inclusion case

Most micro and small enterprises fail not because the underlying business is unsound, but because of short-term timing mismatches between outflows and inflows. Chikwama Alert gives NBM relationship managers early, consented visibility into which clients are approaching a cash gap — days or weeks before it happens — turning reactive loan applications into proactive, data-backed facility offers. This builds a lending relationship on transaction history rather than collateral alone, which is exactly the kind of inclusive underwriting model most under-40 Malawian entrepreneurs currently fall outside of.

Chikwama Alert — built for the National Virtual Hackathon for Financial Innovation in Malawi (FINOVATE 2026), COSSIS · MUBAS, sponsored by National Bank of Malawi plc. This is a working UI/data prototype with simulated transaction and forecast data; production use would require explicit customer consent for mobile-money data access under applicable Malawian data protection rules.
